Volumetric Color Grading

This demo applies color grading inside the raymarching loop, creating volumetric fog effects with custom color transformations.

Each raymarch step accumulates color through the grading LUT, allowing for atmospheric effects like colored fog, depth-based color shifts, and volumetric tinting.

Key difference: Color grading happens at every raymarch iteration, not just at the final surface hit. This creates depth-dependent color effects.

Adjust fog density and start distance to control the volumetric effect intensity. Use mouse to orbit the camera.
