Research Benchmark:
• 1.0M particles rendering in real-time
• 10 connected Bezier segments forming ONE continuous curve
• Particles flow sequentially through the entire curve
• Each particle evaluates curve position every frame
Seiler's Method:
Leverages GPU texture filtering hardware to perform lerp operations. Complexity is O(1) - constant time regardless of segment count!
Traditional Method (Fixed):
Uses BufferAttributes instead of uniforms. Each particle has its own control points. No uniform limits, but still O(n) computation.
Based on "Seiler's Interpolation" paper (Lin et al. 2021)
