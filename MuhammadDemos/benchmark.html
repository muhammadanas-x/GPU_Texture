Research Benchmark:

  • 1M particles rendering in real-time
  • 1000 connected Bezier segments forming ONE continuous curve
  • Particles flow sequentially through the entire curve
  • Each particle evaluates curve position every frame

Seiler's Method: Leverages GPU texture filtering hardware to perform lerp operations. Complexity is O(1) - constant time regardless of segment count!

Traditional Method: Uses polynomial calculations per frame. Complexity is O(n) per particle.

Based on "Seiler's Interpolation" paper (Lin et al. 2021)
